## How to Test

1. Open `src/index.css`
2. Find lines 3-7 (primary colors) and line 40 (RGB)
3. Replace with any option above
4. Save and refresh browser
5. All gradients, patterns, and effects update automatically!

## Why These Colors Work

All themes are chosen based on:
- **Color Theory**: Complementary or harmonious with red logo
- **Psychology**: Evoke confidence, trust, and positivity
- **Professional**: Premium, sophisticated feel
- **Accessibility**: Good contrast ratios maintained
